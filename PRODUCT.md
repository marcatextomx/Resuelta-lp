# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS generated by `build.mjs` (Node, no dependencies) from `src/` into `dist/`. Deployed on Firebase Hosting (`firebase.json`).

## Users

Owners, general directors, HR and administration of companies in Hermosillo, Sonora, with staff registered at the IMSS. Two states of mind: preventive (wants things in order) and urgent (citatorio, demand, STPS inspection, a dismissal this week). Workers are not clients; the site says so plainly.

## Product Purpose

Website of Resuelta, a labour-law firm that only represents employers. It must rank for "abogados laborales para empresas Hermosillo" and each service's search, be quotable by AI answers, and get the visitor to start the free labour diagnosis (7 questions, sent by WhatsApp).

## Positioning

Preventive labour law implemented inside the company (contracts, Reglamento Interior de Trabajo, comisiones mixtas, protocolos, STPS and REPSE), plus defence from conciliation to trial. Calm and exact where competitors use alarm.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

1. Exact over loud: facts with their article of the LFT, no fear marketing.
2. One action: the free diagnosis.
3. Findable and quotable: every page opens with a plain answer paragraph.
4. Honest proof only.
